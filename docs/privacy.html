Your diary stays on your device

You choose what to record: food and drinks, ingredients, symptoms and positive feelings, dates, symptom severity, stress, sleep, notes and daily reviews. Bellywise stores your diary on your iPhone or iPad and calculates its exploratory patterns there. You do not need an account. Bellywise does not upload your diary to a developer-operated server and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

The native app uses a SQLCipher-encrypted database. A random encryption key is held in the device's secure key store and is available only while the device is unlocked. It is configured to remain on that device. Encryption depends on your device's security, passcode and operating-system protections; it is not a guarantee against access to an unlocked or compromised device.

Bellywise does not provide cloud synchronisation or a server copy of your diary. Your operating system may include app files in its own backups. A device-bound encryption key may not transfer with those files. Before changing or resetting devices, save a Bellywise backup from My space and store it privately.

Camera, photos and ingredient recognition

Camera access is optional. You can scan a product barcode or photograph an ingredients list. Ingredient text recognition uses Apple Vision on the device. Bellywise does not upload label photos or camera video. You can also choose an existing photo or enter ingredients manually.

A label photo is temporarily held in the app's cache while you choose the ingredients area. Bellywise attempts to remove that temporary copy when processing finishes, you dismiss the form, or recognition fails. This does not remove an original picture from your photo library. The ingredient text you save becomes part of your local diary.

Optional online product information

Scanning a barcode requests its product record from Open Food Facts. Choosing an online product search sends the search term, brand or barcode and any selected market to Open Food Facts. These requests include ordinary internet connection information, such as your IP address, request time and the app's user-agent. They do not include your diary, symptoms, notes or label photos. Do not include personal or medical details in a product-search field.

Open Food Facts operates its own service and can retain connection and request logs under its own policy. Open Food Facts' published policy lists retention of visitor IP addresses and logs for three years for security, technical analysis and popularity measurement. Bellywise cannot delete the provider's records. You can use manual food entry and local ingredient recognition without an online product lookup.

Notifications and the Home Screen widget

If you enable reminders, Bellywise schedules them locally on your device at the times you choose. New-pattern notifications are also generated locally when the app checks your diary. Bellywise does not obtain a remote push token or register your diary with an Expo notification server. Notification text can mention an ingredient and feeling, so it may reveal private information on your lock screen depending on your iOS settings. You can disable alerts in Bellywise or iOS Settings.

The Home Screen widget provides shortcuts into the app. It does not display your diary or send its contents to a server.

Exports, backups and other websites

You control whether to export a report or backup, and where to save or share it. Exported reports and JSON backups contain readable diary data and do not have the app database's encryption. Your chosen destination, such as Files, an email service or cloud storage, controls that copy. Bellywise attempts to delete its temporary export or import cache file after the operation ends.

When you open an educational source or product page, your browser and that website receive ordinary connection information and apply their own privacy practices. Visiting Bellywise support or privacy pages likewise involves the website host's normal connection logging.

The Bellywise support and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. These pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts or a contact form. GitHub processes normal connection information when you visit, under its privacy statement.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the provider receives the address, message and attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request and investigate the issue, and is handled through the provider’s email service. Share only what is needed. Use fictional diary entries or remove sensitive details from screenshots where possible. You can contact the privacy address above about access, correction, deletion and retention of information you have sent to support.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Diary entries remain locally until you edit or delete them, reset the diary, or remove the app's stored files. My space provides export, backup and diary-deletion controls. Starting a fresh diary after a storage error preserves the older unreadable database so it is not overwritten. Deleting the active diary does not delete separately preserved files, operating-system backups, exports or copies you have shared. Bellywise cannot recover records from a developer server because no diary server exists.

You can refuse camera access and notifications and continue using manual entry. You can avoid online lookups. For requests concerning third-party service logs, contact that service using its policy above. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict personal information, object to certain processing or complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact the privacy address above for information held by the Bellywise provider; the provider cannot access your device's diary unless you choose to send it.

Audience and changes

Bellywise is intended as an adult diary and educational tool. It does not diagnose intolerance, allergy or another medical condition and is not intended to manage children's diets. If these data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date.
