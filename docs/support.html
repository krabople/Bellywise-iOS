Reporting a problem

Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, Bellywise version and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help. Please remove private diary details or use the example diary where possible.

Common questions

Getting started

Open My space and choose the getting-started guide to replay the tutorial. You can also explore the example diary, which is kept separate from your personal records.

Log food and drinks quickly

Choose Log food or drink to scan a packet's barcode, photograph its ingredients, search by brand, or type a food name. If the barcode service cannot provide ingredients, photograph the ingredients list or enter them manually. Check the exact product and current packet: online records and common recipe suggestions can be incomplete or out of date. You can edit or remove ingredients before saving.

Leftovers and repeat meals

Use a previous meal or choose Log again beside a saved entry. Check the date, time and ingredients for this occasion. Group items under breakfast, lunch or dinner to keep a meal together.

Record how you feel

Choose Log a feeling, select a symptom or positive feeling, and enter when it happened and how strong it was. You can add your own feelings and symptoms. The timestamp helps distinguish symptoms before a food from symptoms after it.

Review each day

Daily context adds stress, sleep and useful notes. If there were no symptoms, explicitly confirm this in the daily review. A day with entries but no completed review is unfinished: its recorded events remain in the diary, but missing foods or symptoms cannot reliably be treated as absent. Use the unfinished-days link to find and review those days.

Understand a pattern

Patterns are exploratory associations, not proof that a food caused a symptom. Open a pattern to see the days counted, comparison groups, severity, timeline and meals. A following-day pattern uses the food day and the next calendar day's feelings. Different ingredients can have different eligible days. Regular logging and completed reviews make comparisons more useful; it can take time to gather enough records.

Reminders and Home Screen shortcuts

My space lets you choose food reminders, day-review reminders and local new-pattern alerts. Check notification permission in iOS Settings if alerts do not appear. You can add the medium Bellywise quick-log widget through the iOS Home Screen widget gallery; its shortcuts open the relevant forms in the app.

Keep a backup

In My space, choose Save a backup before moving devices, resetting the app or uninstalling it. Backups and exported reports contain readable diary data, so save and share them privately. Restoring a backup replaces the current diary; save a copy first. Bellywise has no server recovery service. If you see a storage error, do not uninstall before preserving any data you need. Contact support with the exact error and whether it is a first install or a device transfer.

Privacy

Your diary and pattern analysis stay on the device. Online product requests and external websites have separate privacy practices. Read the Bellywise privacy policy for details about local storage, camera processing, optional product lookups, notifications and exports.

Health questions

Bellywise support can help with the app, but cannot diagnose or give individual medical advice. Discuss persistent or concerning symptoms with a qualified clinician. Consult a clinician before making significant dietary changes, and never use an app pattern to test a suspected allergy or decide that a food is safe. For a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services.
