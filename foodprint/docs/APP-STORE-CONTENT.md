## Description

Meet Bellywise, a thoughtful place to record what you eat and how you feel.

Build a food and symptom diary that fits your day. Add meals in your own words, review suggested ingredients, and record symptoms or positive experiences with a few taps. Choose from common symptoms or add your own.

MAKE INGREDIENTS CLEARER

Scan an ingredients panel with your camera, then check and edit the recognised text before saving. Search packaged products through Open Food Facts when you choose to connect. Product information and common recipe suggestions can be incomplete, so you stay in control of the ingredients in your diary.

EXPLORE YOUR PATTERNS

See exploratory associations between your logged ingredients and symptoms. Bellywise explains why results need context and when more observations are needed. Consistent check-ins, including comfortable days, help you build a fuller record.

LEARN AS YOU GO

Read accessible guides to intolerance and allergy, lactose, wheat and gluten, FODMAPs, label reading and useful next steps, with links to the original health sources.

KEEP YOUR DIARY CLOSE

Your diary and analysis stay on your device. There are no advertising trackers or required accounts. Optional product searches send the search text or barcode to Open Food Facts. You decide when to export and share your records.

Bellywise is a diary and educational tool. It has not been clinically validated and cannot diagnose an intolerance, allergy or other medical condition. Associations do not establish cause. Consult a qualified clinician about persistent symptoms and before making significant dietary changes. Never use Bellywise to test a known or suspected allergy or to decide whether a food is safe for you.

## TestFlight beta description

Bellywise helps adults keep a food and symptom diary and explore possible patterns. This beta focuses on clear logging, editable ingredient recognition, useful symptom check-ins and cautious, explainable analysis. The app is not clinically validated or intended to diagnose or treat a medical condition.

## What to test

Please try logging ordinary meals and a few packaged products. Compare recognised ingredients with the full label, including a gluten-free or lactose-free example. Check that you can correct ingredients, add a custom symptom, record a comfortable check-in, edit or remove an entry, and return to the same diary after restarting the app.

Review the diary and pattern explanations for clarity. Let us know about crashes, text that is hard to read, confusing dates, camera problems or ingredients that look wrong. Use fictional records if you do not want to share health information in screenshots or feedback. Do not change your diet or deliberately provoke symptoms to test this beta.

## App Review notes

Bellywise is an observational diary with general health education. Its exploratory association engine is not clinically validated and does not diagnose disease. No login is needed. Health guidance links to primary public sources. The app advises users to consult a clinician and does not provide self-directed allergy challenges or prescribe an elimination diet.

Camera recognition runs on the device; users review extracted ingredients before saving. Optional Open Food Facts lookup is a network feature initiated by the user. Diary storage and analysis are local. Export creates a file only at the user's request. The browser demonstration is not the submitted iOS app.
