# Data sources and licences

Contains information from [Open Food Facts contributors](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/), available under the [Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

The following adapted databases are distributed under ODbL 1.0. Complete, unrestricted machine-readable copies are available free of charge:

- [Ingredient taxonomy JSON](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/krabople/Bellywise-iOS/main/foodprint/src/data/openFoodFactsIngredients.json).
- [Brand taxonomy JSON](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/krabople/Bellywise-iOS/main/foodprint/src/data/openFoodFactsBrands.json).
- [Food guide with ingredient mappings JSON](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/krabople/Bellywise-iOS/main/foodprint/src/data/extendedFoodGuide.json). The USDA source descriptions are public domain / CC0; the adapted ingredient-mapping database uses Open Food Facts taxonomy and Bellywise curated entries and is distributed under ODbL 1.0.

Raw USDA data in `usdaFoodDescriptions.json`, `brandCatalog.json` and `brandedProducts.json` are public domain / CC0. Attribution: U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service, Beltsville Human Nutrition Research Center. FoodData Central. [Internet]. [cited 7 October 2026]. Available from [FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/).

Database licences cover these data, separately from application software and privately stored user diary entries. Recipe mappings are estimates, not manufacturer-confirmed product recipes. Online Open Food Facts product results retain their source link and attribution in the app.

The public [data sources and licences notice](https://krabople.github.io/Bellywise-iOS/data-sources.html) is linked from My space and the privacy sheet. No copied restaurant menu snapshots or direct restaurant ingredient APIs are distributed in this release. Restaurant aliases identify brands for optional Open Food Facts searches; they do not claim a complete menu or affiliation.
